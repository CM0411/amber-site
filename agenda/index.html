Novera Agenda

A calendar that thinks about the road. For every appointment with an address you see when to leave, including the chance of a traffic jam.

Help and contact

A question, a bug or an idea? Email info@novera-ai.nl. You will get an answer directly from the maker.

Frequently asked questions

How does the app know when I should leave?
The app works out the driving time from where you are, with Apple Maps. The chance of traffic jams and delays along the way comes from the traffic data of Novera Route.

Do I get a reminder?
Yes, if you want one. You get a notification when it is time to go.

Is the app free?
Yes, and it has no ads.

Do I need an account?
No.

Privacy

We never sell your data and never share it with anyone. The full privacy policy will be published here before the app appears in the App Store.

Sources

Traffic data: NDW and Rijkswaterstaat. Map and driving time: Apple Maps.
